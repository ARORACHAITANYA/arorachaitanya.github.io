How I work

Correctness is the job

Before I build, I pin the requirement down one question at a time until the edge cases are explicit, and I write the expected behaviour down first. I measure before I argue. When a service falls over under load, I profile it until I know where the time goes; when a design is contested, I build a test rig and bring numbers.

I keep designs small and don't repeat checks the system already guarantees. I'm sceptical of any claim, from a teammate's report, a library's default or an AI agent, until I've seen the evidence. I use AI coding agents a lot, as a supervised team rather than an autopilot: every change is checked against the requirement before it merges.

And I write decisions down, in design records, contracts and load-test reports, so the next engineer doesn't have to rediscover them.
